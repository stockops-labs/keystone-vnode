How to stake with Keystone

  1. Copy the validator address above. It's the only thing that identifies this node; ignore any lookalike address you're sent.
  2. Open vApp and go to staking, where you choose a vNode to cooperate with.
  3. Paste the address and check it ends in ….
  4. Enter how much VTRS to stake, review it, and sign.
  5. Your stake starts counting once the next validator selection takes effect, usually within an era or two (eras are 4 hours). Rewards arrive as VNRG energy and are paid out in batches.

Screens in vApp may differ slightly between versions. If anything doesn't match, ask in the official Vitreus community before signing.
